Our story

A trusted partner for building, property and trade

ASTAN International (Pvt) Ltd is a registered Sri Lankan private limited company (Reg. No. PV 00348027) headquartered in Thalawathugoda.

We were founded on a simple idea: clients should not have to manage a long list of separate contractors, consultants and suppliers to get a project done well. By bringing MEP engineering, electrical consultancy, interior design and construction, real estate, international trade and investment under one roof, we make projects simpler, faster and more reliable.

Our work serves homeowners, businesses, developers and international partners who value honest advice, quality workmanship and on-time delivery.

Our Mission

To deliver integrated engineering, interior, property and trade solutions that create lasting value for our clients — through quality workmanship, transparent dealings and dependable service.

Our Vision

To be a leading Sri Lankan company recognised at home and abroad for excellence in building services, interiors, real estate and international trade.
